Every habit app I tried started from a blank list and an infinite streak. Blank lists I abandon. Infinite streaks I fail.

<Shot src="/projects/habitus/habits.png" alt="Habitus: four habits, 21 days each" width="phone" />

Habitus is 21 days, on purpose, with a library of routines other people are already building — so you steal one instead of inventing one. Flutter first, then Swift, then a web companion.

<Shot src="/projects/habitus/modal.png" alt="Adding a habit, a category, or exporting" width="phone" />

The cap is the product. A finish line I can hit. The crowd-sourced library only works if enough people are in it, which is the cold-start problem I never really solved.

The interface was the barrier. If someone couldn’t speak, hear, or see, the phone still could.

<Shot src="/projects/practikality/demo.jpg" alt="A slide from the Imagine Cup showcase" />

Practikality is a three-tier Android app so people who are mute, deaf, or visually impaired can talk to each other — speech, signs, text, and the glue in between. Imagine Cup world finals. I was a teenager. There’s a [video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VhdyqzMQOGE).

The glue was the real problem. Three modes is easy to demo and hard to make feel like one conversation. I wouldn’t ship it the same way now. I’m still glad it exists.

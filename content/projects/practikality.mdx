The interface was the barrier. If someone couldn’t speak, hear, or see, the phone still could.

<Shot src="/shots/practikality/demo.jpg" alt="A slide from the Imagine Cup showcase" />

Practikality is a three-tier Android app so people who are mute, deaf, or visually impaired can talk to each other — speech, signs, text, and the glue in between. We took it to Imagine Cup. World finals. I was a teenager. There’s a [video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VhdyqzMQOGE). Microsoft and YourStory wrote it down; those clips live on the [press](/links) list.

The glue was the real problem. Three modes is easy to demo and hard to make feel like one conversation. The stack was Android because that’s what I had. I wouldn’t ship it the same way now. I’m still glad it exists.

I’d leave the desk with a fleet of coding sessions still running on the Mac, and they were gone until I sat down again.

<Shot src="/projects/mission-control/home.png" alt="Mission Control home: sessions grouped by project" width="phone" />

Mission Control is an iPhone remote for that fleet — native Swift, over Tailscale. What’s running, what’s stuck, what I want to say next. Not a terminal I enjoy using. A remote for a specific job.

<Shot src="/projects/mission-control/session.png" alt="A session on the phone, with a prompt to Claude" width="phone" />

The hard part is the trust boundary. Tailscale is how the phone reaches the Mac; tmux is how the sessions stay alive. Everything else is just a surface on top of those two facts.

I wanted to see a Compose theme the way a design tool shows a library — live, in the running app, not in a screenshot.

<Shot src="/projects/biblioteca/theme.svg" alt="A live theme: colors, buttons, chips" />

Biblioteca is an add-on. Drop it in a module, open a screen of your components, poke the theme at runtime.

It’s a developer toy. Change a color, watch every button, decide if you meant it. That loop is the whole point; it was never going to be a design-system product.

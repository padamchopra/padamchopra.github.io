I don’t trust most messengers with anything I actually care about. “Encrypted in transit” still means readable on a server.

<Shot src="/projects/whisperz/logo.png" alt="Whisperz mark" width="panel" />

Whisperz is a shielded chat — Android and iOS. The Android side leans on Zcash-style shielding. Private means the server cannot read it.

That’s also the trap. Onboarding, notifications, “who is this person” — all the product things people expect — have to live with a server that is not allowed to know. I still haven’t made those feel easy.

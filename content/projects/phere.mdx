Indian weddings were living in WhatsApp groups and Excel. I wanted one room for that chaos.

<Shot src="/projects/phere/site.png" alt="Phere landing: the whole wedding, finally in one calm place" />

Phere is a wedding workspace — guests, vendors, budget, checklist — for the couple, the family, and the planner. The first screen is supposed to answer “what needs attention now?”, not list every aunt equally.

The catch is coordination. Many roles, one date that will not move, and a lot of state that used to live in people’s heads. Making those surfaces know each other without turning into a dashboard is the actual product.
